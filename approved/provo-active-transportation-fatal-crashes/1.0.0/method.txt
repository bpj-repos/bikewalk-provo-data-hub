One feature represents one reviewed fatal crash in the managed active-transportation series. The state portal must assign the crash to Provo and support active-transportation involvement. The hub uses portal person injury level 5 for deaths and checks any corresponding UDOT death count. Unassigned city records are outside coverage. A new or mixed category among the people killed requires review before publication. The geojson omits personal attributes, exact times and dates, police case numbers, news and narratives. The 2010–2017 archive has been examined separately and is outside this version. State records may be revised.
