One feature represents one state-recorded fatal active transportation crash assigned to Provo. The 2010–2017 source is a separate all-severity Provo portal export. From 2018 the source is the managed collection. Both use the same state ID, Mountain Time occurrence month, portal coordinates rounded to four decimals, and person records (injury level 5) for the death count and mode. Compare any UDOT count in the same group when available. Older crash-sheet involvement flags are blank; the Rollups table and person types preserve active transportation involvement. This release does not treat missing flags as negative. Older vehicle labels, road functional class, person circumstances and demographic completeness differ across periods. The layer supports comparing the published measures for recorded crashes. It cannot establish changes in exposure, reporting completeness or risk. The archival eight each have a portal fatal severity and one corresponding pedestrian or pedalcyclist death. Independent UDOT counts were unavailable for those eight. State records may be revised.
