# Interpreting the series

One row represents a source field at a station in a collection year. Totals overlap session counts and recorded F/M breakdowns. Select a single slot, mode and breakdown for summation. The unit is observations. Station IDs are local to each year/source layer; a matching number does not establish an identical crossing line.

The 2026 protocol counts passages across a designated line. Session 1 and session 2 correspond to the source weekday and Saturday fields. For historical exports the weekday column gives the source label where available, not proof of actual attendance. The 2019 workbook documents Thursday September 12 (5–7 pm) and Saturday September 14 (noon–2 pm). Dates and 120-minute windows appear only for populated reconciled workbook sessions. Other durations are unknown. Scheduled 2026 volunteer times do not establish actual observation duration. Combined values are source-reported. The 2026 combined scooter column sums to 236; the session scooter columns sum to 283. Preserve both measures.

A zero with observed status is a stored count. Missing is a source null. Unresolved indicates a withheld value, including a nonnull value without its session total, a workbook conflict or a recorded breakdown that fails reconciliation. Measures absent from a year's mapping are unavailable for that year and generate no invented rows. F and M represent source-recorded categories. No personal characteristics can be inferred about individuals from these station aggregates.

The known series covers 2019 and 2021–2026; there is no 2020 file in the verified holdings. Source coordinates, private signup records, individual data, weather, extrapolated estimates and workbook percentages are outside the release. Differences in session timing, crossing lines, station sets and category coverage limit historical comparisons. Citation is encouraged. CC0 applies to dataset rights held by BikeWalk; the source workbook, maps and slides are not redistributed here.
