# Provo crash intersection and street-segment assignments

This companion package contains one row per managed nonmotorist crash group after reconciliation and summary review. It uses Provo City street geometry, not PeopleForBikes geometry. Count distinct crashId. A matched row has assignmentType=intersection or segment and exactly one corresponding identity. An intersection's relatedSegmentIds describe its approaches; they do not allocate that crash to every approach. Ambiguous source identities remain separate groups and cannot receive confirmed assignments. State sourceRecords support joins; private police case identifiers, addresses, notes and narratives are not exported.

## Scope and inventory

The managed collection extends beyond Provo. Rows outside the municipal polygon have status=out-of-scope and scope=outside-provo, and are excluded from inProvoStatuses and exceptions.json. Located Provo rows have scope=inside-provo. Unlocated rows have unknown scope and remain a separate review population. This is not the Portal city-assignment annual population.

Capture every City Parcel/FeatureServer/0 feature intersecting the municipal boundary envelope through the maintained complete-ID acquisition helper. The boundary comes from the latest approved bicycle traffic-stress package; that package contributes no road geometry. OBJECTID is unique only within a captured source version. STREETID, LOCAL_UID and UNIQUE_ID are not reliable segment identities. Pin the package version and manifest checksum whenever storing segmentId or intersectionId.

segments.geojson preserves original full geometry and every reviewed City attribute under properties.source, including nulls, aliases, classification, ownership, lanes, speed, address ranges, facility and maintenance attributes. UPDATEDBY is excluded because it identifies staff editors. New or removed schema fields fail preparation for review. dictionary.json preserves field aliases, types and domains. City measurements remain unchanged; undocumented units stay unresolved. Native Shape__Length uses EPSG:3566 (Utah Central US survey feet). Derived lengths use EPSG:26912 metres. Current attributes are not historical crash-date conditions.

All source rows remain, including zero-crash and ineligible roads. Eligible means positive within-Provo length, not Planned and not Walkway. Private roads are retained; publicOwner is separate from eligibility. Consumers must disclose ownership/class filters in mileage denominators. Provo's captured metadata supplies no specific open license: retain original attribution and informational-use limitations rather than inventing reuse rights.

## Length and intersection identities

Use withinBoundaryLengthMeters once per eligible physicalGeometryId; divide by 1609.344 for miles. Exact reversed copies of the same complete geometry and recorded grade share a key. Divided carriageways, ramps, frontage roads and differently digitized lines remain separate. This is source-line mileage, not lane-miles or deduplicated corridor mileage. No corridor aggregation is provided.

intersections.geojson combines derived City junctions with the Hub’s latest approved UDOT ATSPM intersection roster. A roster site is used only when both named roads are corroborated by City lines within 45 m; ramp meters are excluded. Matching City junctions use the provider site identity while retaining City junction geometry and component IDs. Original provider coordinates remain separate; they can identify a controller position rather than the geometric street junction. The complete approved sites resource is included as intersection-sites.geojson, and its release pin participates in normal update invalidation. Derived identities are explicitly distinguished from providerSiteId. A City endpoint junction requires at least three physical arms, including T junctions where an endpoint meets a line interior within 0.5 m. A two-arm bend or name change is not a junction. Grade disagreement prevents linking known different levels. Nearby nodes with the same complete street-name combination, a shared connecting segment and a maximum 20 m separation can form one intersection; generic Access Road nodes are not merged this way. componentIntersectionIds preserve those original nodes. Each intersection includes its names, geometry, constituent segmentIds, sourceVersion, grade and derivation basis.

Interior crossings are retained only as unconfirmed candidates and require crash junction evidence before assignment; different known grades never form a candidate. An unknown-grade interior crossing requires two corroborated road names, or a source-classified intersection within 15 m. Geometry alone does not establish that a bridge connects to the street beneath it. The City snapshot lacks grade throughout; neither endpoint coincidence nor these assignments prove routing connectivity. endpoints.json and coverage.confirmedConnectivity preserve that limitation.

## Matching rule 2

Prefer original Portal coordinates, including attached Portal observations, then UDOT, then retained police geocodes. Matching uses retained precision; exported crash coordinates are rounded to four decimals. State coordinate disagreement exceeding 50 m and unresolved state identity block confirmation. A police-only record may use its stored map-eligible geocode and normalized address when the source has no conflict; only recognized City street names are exported as evidence, never the private address or notes. This does not claim a geocoded address is the precise collision point.

Normalize punctuation, directional abbreviations, numeric street suffixes, attached address directions, interstate spellings and explicitly enumerated transcription errors. Match longest street phrases before shorter aliases, ignore bare direction/type tokens and distinguish an address's numeric prefix from its primary road. Trailing business/dispatch wording cannot become a new street. Historical Bulldog/Cougar aliases are documented by [Provo's retained public report](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/files/566500.pdf); City originals remain unchanged. Original state road descriptions and normalized matching evidence remain available separately. No general fuzzy-name substitution overrides contradictory coordinates.

Segments require a corroborated road within 25 m, without a different physical line within 8 m of its distance or competing endpoints within 12 m. A state record with only an incomplete numeric location can use a unique line within 5 m, labeled matchEvidence=recorded-location-only. That fallback cannot override a recognized different road, off-road description or private geocode. Other matched rows use road-and-location evidence. Consumers can exclude location-only rows from stricter analyses.

Source-classified intersections/roundabouts, explicit junction descriptions and two recognized spaced street names trigger intersection matching. Search up to 60 m; a single-road description is limited to 35 m. Every recognized primary road must agree with the candidate; an address-grid prefix cannot substitute for a conflicting primary road. Competing equally supported intersections within 12 m of the selected distance remain ambiguous. Without source junction evidence, a uniquely supported endpoint junction within 12 m, or one corroborated by two street/grid names within 15 m, can receive a spatial junction assignment. Reasons distinguish source-reported and spatially inferred junctions. Intersection distance is to its representative point, not a road edge.

One matched crash receives one intersection or one segment identity. Intersection approaches are contextual links only. All alternative candidates, distances, name agreement, source classification and identity uncertainty remain visible. These are screening rules, not independent ground-truth validation or an inference of fault.

## Exceptions, rebuilding and recovery

exceptions.json lists every unresolved in-scope or unlocated group, with category, reasons and nearest-named-segment diagnostics where available. Categories distinguish missing locations, private geocode review, off-road/parking descriptions, recognized road/location disagreement, position offsets, competing geometries, unresolved intersections and identity conflicts. Numeric distances in this diagnostic are calculated from retained positions. A source correction or new matching evidence must rerun the maintained pipeline; no consumer should silently replace an unresolved row with its nearest street.

The normal scripts.update_crashes path captures City data before online crash intake, runs reconciliation and summary review, then prepares all segment/intersection/exception resources together. Pending summaries block preparation. Offline summary application reuses the verified capture, and offline/no-op crash updates still check association dependencies. The standalone scripts.crash_road_segments command supports verified isolated rehearsals. No new schedule is introduced.

Fingerprints bind the full collection, City features and metadata, approved intersection roster, boundary, rules, producer/name/capture helpers and environment declaration. Unchanged recipes verify existing resources and ZIP before returning unchanged. Changed dependencies produce a new content-addressed private candidate. Locks, input rechecks and atomic writes preserve the last successful candidate on failure. A changed boundary envelope requires recapture. Crash rollback does not silently rewrite association pins: rerun the builder before adopting the rollback.

review.json reports crash assignment/evidence changes, new/removed/changed intersection identities and source segment geometry, attributes and lengths. Splits, merges and renumbering trigger full reassignment rather than guessed lineage. Reproducing a comparison package requires its exact comparison baseline as well as pinned inputs. Candidate preparation uses the maintained catalog ZIP routine; every manifest resource, including intersections and exceptions, is in the same archive. Approved packages are immutable. Exact-pin owner review, ledger registration and staged/live public byte/header verification remain separate from preparation. Consumers adopt independently. Release 1.0.0 and later approved ledger entries supersede the original candidate hold. New candidate versions still require the standard verification and publication workflow.


## Materialized crash-record enrichment

`crash-records.json` supplies one minimized record per managed group: opaque `crashId`, source-qualified state `sourceRecords`, and `roadLocation`. The Hub performs the association and metadata joins during every normal update. `roadLocation` contains assignment type, intersection/segment/physical geometry IDs, related approach IDs, status, scope, identity uncertainty, match evidence, exception category/reasons, distance and rule version. Resolved intersections carry names, representative coordinates, provider/derivation basis and component IDs. Resolved segments carry names, lengths in metres, geometry identity, eligibility, ownership, grade/connectivity uncertainty and the reviewed City attributes. An unresolved record has null intersection/segment metadata; proximity is not silently promoted to an assignment. No private crash attributes or police identifiers are added to this public resource.

The same stage produces a checksummed private derived collection with `enrichment.roadLocation` on every complete crash record, preserving observations, reviewed summaries and group IDs. The structured updater result exposes it as `stages.roadSegments.enrichedCollection`; its source collection checksum and road-package manifest checksum must both match before adoption. The private view lives outside the public package. It does not replace or mutate the original reconciled release, change its snapshot identity, or participate in summary invalidation. Road-only changes rebuild the view and package without rewriting crash evidence. Candidate pointer replacement occurs only after both outputs verify; failures retain the previous pointer.

Consumers can copy the prepared `roadLocation` object instead of joining assignments to feature tables themselves. The approved package manifest supplies its version and provenance. Source-qualified IDs that identify multiple groups remain ambiguous; consumers must not collapse them. Dashboard labels, selected populations, rankings and UI caches remain consumer responsibilities. Intersection approaches never allocate extra crashes. Road attributes describe the retained City snapshot, not historical conditions on the crash date.
