# Crash associations with Provo City street segments

This companion package contains every managed nonmotorist crash group after source reconciliation, including unlocated and out-of-boundary records. It is not the Portal city-assignment annual population. Count distinct crashId, and only status=matched for single-segment totals. Ambiguous source identities remain separate groups, carry identityStatus=ambiguous-state-id and cannot receive status=matched. They must not be silently combined or counted as confirmed distinct crashes. State sourceRecords support consumer joins; police case identifiers, original narratives and staff editor IDs are not public resources. Original observations remain immutable in the private managed release.

## Backbone and complete attributes

Use Provo City GIS, Parcel/FeatureServer/0, Provo Streets. Capture every feature intersecting the approved municipal boundary envelope through the maintained complete-ID acquisition helper. The boundary is the checksummed boundary resource of the latest approved bicycle traffic-stress package; that package supplies no street geometry to this association. The City layer's OBJECTID identifies a feature within a captured version. STREETID, LOCAL_UID and UNIQUE_ID are not unique segment keys. A source-qualified segmentId always requires the package/version pin.

segments.geojson retains the complete original geometry and every reviewed City property under properties.source, including nulls, name/alias components, owner/classification, lane/speed fields, one-way/vertical level fields, road-address ranges, facility fields and maintenance attributes. Only UPDATEDBY is excluded because it identifies staff editors. New or removed source fields fail preparation until reviewed. dictionary.json retains source aliases, types and coded domains. Source-native measurements remain untouched; undocumented units remain unresolved. Native Shape__Length uses the source spatial reference (EPSG:3566, Utah Central US survey feet); derived lengths are independently computed in EPSG:26912 metres. Do not infer missing direction, speed, lanes or grade from a road class. Provo supplies no specific open license in the captured metadata: retain attribution and informational-use/accuracy limitations; this package does not grant rights beyond the original source.

All captured source segments remain in the download, including zero-crash roads and ineligible rows. A road is eligible when it intersects the municipal polygon with positive line length, is not Planned and is not a Walkway. Private roads are retained and marked by their original OWNER; publicOwner is a separate convenience flag. Consumers must disclose ownership/class filters in a mileage denominator. Names are normalized only in derived.names; source values are unchanged.

## Length and topology

Full and within-boundary lengths are in metres, computed in UTM 12N from source geometry; divide by 1609.344 for international miles. Use withinBoundaryLengthMeters once per distinct eligible physicalGeometryId. Exact reversed/forward copies of the same complete geometry and recorded vertical level share that key. This is physical source-line mileage, not directional lane-miles. Divided carriageways, ramps, frontage roads and parallel paths remain separate source lines. Partial overlaps and differently digitized parallel centerlines are not silently dissolved; mileage is not a deduplicated corridor-centerline measure. Connected corridor aggregation is outside this release.

The City snapshot has no endpoint node identifiers and its VERT_LEVEL values are missing. Derived endpointIds cluster source line ends to 0.1 m and recorded level. They express potential endpoint coincidence, not confirmed traversability. No intersection is created where line interiors geometrically cross. Bridge/tunnel connections cannot be established without grade evidence. Multipart geometry retains all component endpoints. endpoints.json lists these candidate relationships and coverage.json explicitly marks connectivity unconfirmed. Empty City FROM_ROAD/TO_ROAD and ONEWAY fields remain empty.

## Assignment rule 1

Prefer original Portal coordinates, including attached Portal observations, then UDOT, then police geocodes. Preserve source disagreement. Match the recorded Portal road name or UDOT main-road field to City names/aliases using the established street-name normalization. Search within 25 m in EPSG:26912; proximity without a corroborated name never produces a match. Retain all spatial candidates and their distances, endpoint proximity, name agreement, eligibility and recorded grade.

A supported candidate must have no competing physical segment within 8 m of its distance, and no alternative segment endpoint within 12 m when the supported endpoint is also that near. Nearby ineligible linework (including parallel walkways) also remains competing evidence rather than being silently ignored. Such junction/parallel alternatives remain ambiguous even if one line is nearest. A source-classified intersection or roundabout, or an explicit junction in the recorded road description, remains ambiguous even if only one nearby line qualifies. Source coordinate disagreement over 50 m and police-only locations also remain ambiguous when a supported candidate exists. Uncorroborated/missing roads are unmatched; invalid or absent coordinates are missing-location. Outside-boundary crashes are unmatched with an explicit reason. These tolerances are screening rules, not survey accuracy or proof of causal road involvement. Historical crash positions may be approximate and current City segments may postdate the crash.

Original state junction classifications remain in junctionEvidence, including missing/unknown distinctions. Each row contains status, selected segment/physical key or null, ruleVersion, reasons, candidate evidence and rounded public coordinates. Every assigned segment's complete approved source metadata is obtainable from the same ZIP. No additional live service is needed. Junction alternatives are retained without allocating a crash fractionally or inventing a single road.

## Rebuild, change and recovery

The normal scripts.update_crashes path captures the City dependency on online runs, performs existing crash intake/reconciliation and required summary review, then rebuilds this package. Offline summary application reuses that captured source. A pending summary queue prevents the association stage. Explicit scripts.crash_road_segments can rebuild a verified source receipt, or recapture online, without changing crash observations or consumer pins.

Dependency fingerprints bind the complete crash collection, full City feature bytes and metadata, boundary, rules, producer/name helpers and environment declaration. An unchanged recipe verifies every existing package file and returns unchanged. Changed dependencies produce a new content-addressed private candidate. Atomic writes and a filesystem lock preserve the last successful association candidate on failure. A new boundary envelope requires online recapture. A stale candidate must not be adopted: compare its dependencies.crashCollection with the active pointer and its remaining dependency hashes with the reviewed inputs. Rollback of the crash collection intentionally does not rewrite associations; rerun the offline association builder before adopting the rolled-back state.

review.json reports added/removed crash and source IDs, reassignment, geometry/length and attribute changes. Renumbering, splitting and merging trigger full reassignment; no provider ID or prior match is inherited across changed evidence. This explicit change report is the lineage policy. Earlier approved packages remain immutable. Review real candidates and coverage before adding a new version/pin to the existing release ledger. Candidate preparation uses the maintained catalog builder’s deterministic ZIP routine and verifies the archive again on reuse. The maintained catalog builder creates the complete manifest-listed ZIP, and the normal public verifier checks staged and live bytes/headers. Consumers adopt independently; no chart, consumer pin or new time-based schedule is part of this producer.
