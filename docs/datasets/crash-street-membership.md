# Provo crash street membership 1.0.0

Join membership.json by the opaque crash ID in the Streets Watch snapshot identified by the manifest's crashDataset. Each entry has sorted unique streetIds, status, reasons and evidence. Look up canonical display names in streets.json. Count distinct crash IDs for a selected street or union of streets. One intersection can belong to both streets. The denominator is the 1,358 mapped crash groups on this specific consumer pin. Private collection records outside the map have no row.

Street IDs are scoped to Provo and derived from normalized inventory names, independent of row order and geometry segmentation. The pinned traffic-stress segment inventory supplies names. Cardinal prefixes on grid streets describe the address quadrant. The cardinal direction after the grid number defines the street. Named directional forms share an existing undirected inventory name. The inventory groups corridors by name and makes no claim that all like-named segments are connected. Preserve published IDs when reviewing future renames.

The producer reads the portal Road name and UDOT Main road name. It expands road-type abbreviations, resolves address prefixes, recognizes explicit intersection separators and reviewed compound descriptions, and records source, field and rule for each assignment. The original field is available in the separately pinned crash snapshot. The reviewed UNIV AVE abbreviation adds five distinct crashes to the 231 explicitly spelling University Ave or Avenue. University Parkway is separate. A bare University, a route number, a business or proximity alone does not establish a street. Secondary semicolon annotations can mix route numbers, notes and local aliases and are excluded. No nearest-road inference is applied. Missing police road fields and unidentified names yield unresolved rows. Partial means at least one primary component was identified and another was unresolved. A matched row can still lack an unrecorded cross street.

The Hub owns membership. Consumer adoption is independent. Retrieve the manifest and verify its SHA-256, then verify each listed resource before joining. For reproduction, extract the versioned gzip snapshot from the Streets Watch repository at data/crash-stress/versions/<crashDataset>.json.gz. Run python -m scripts.prepare_crash_streets SNAPSHOT NEW_DIRECTORY and compare all four files. Normal catalog assembly creates the complete ZIP. Roll back by selecting the prior consumer pin and omitting the join.
