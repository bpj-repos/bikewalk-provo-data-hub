# Release-owner decision, November 7, 2026

The owner confirmed the documented 2019 and 2021–2026 series comprises the known holdings, selected the most open reuse terms, and approved the described mappings, coverage and publication under CC0. Recommended attribution is BikeWalk Provo and Provo City active transportation count contributors. Scope is station observations and hub metadata. Each year has 14 source station rows. The dataset contains 1,938 observed values, 281 source nulls and 35 unresolved values withheld. No other years or unverified duration assumptions were added. Coverage is independent of whether comparisons across years are appropriate.

The private station-level workbook review records every named station in the 2019 NBPD Data Sheet, its two session labels, timing, and mode/F-field disagreements with the annual export. Nineteen of 26 listed slots have observed workbook counts. Cougar Boulevard is export-only and has no 2019 session observations. The worksheet's first session is Thursday despite the historical GIS weekday label. Two Saturday bicycle zeros at Center Street have blank workbook cells and missing session totals. At Heritage Halls the first-session workbook pedestrian and other values are 145 and 10; the GIS values are 87 and 7. Withhold these two fields and both overlapping all-mode totals. Station 3's 60 E workbook label differs from the 50 E export label. Counts align but the crossing-line position is uncertain. Preserve the source label and avoid longitudinal identities.

2021 has no pedestrian F/M breakdown. Some stored values lack session totals, and some recorded F/M pairs in 2023–24 fail to reconcile. The complete private withheld-value ledger records the specific year, station, field, original value and reason. Scooter combined and daily counts are valid distinct source fields in 2026; do not substitute one for the other. The 2026 Cougar Boulevard record has both sessions; an earlier missing-session reference cannot establish a different year without evidence. Actual attendance minutes and observer classification procedures are not established by volunteer schedules. Do not infer historical timing or rates.

Rebuild from the canonical source Git commit in sources.json. Each input identifies its pinned path, hash, inventory date, and last source commit date. Raw capture timestamps are unknown where the original sources supply none. Annual CSV/JSON values must agree exactly. Historical coverage notes and the 2026 asset inventory document original ArcGIS service links and collection materials. The historical exporter uses the view through 2022 and the base from 2023; 2026 uses its own hosted layer. For independent workbook review run scripts/audit_counts_sources.py SOURCE_REPO NEW_DIR with xlrd 2.0.2 and compare the generated JSON files to this directory. xlrd is unnecessary for normal builds.

## Publication checks

Use the source-repo environment variable in the README to run real-data tests. Build into a fresh private directory. Only its public/ contents and vercel.json may enter the Vercel staging directory. Keep candidate review issues separate. The real manifest pin guards every included resource; restore prior catalog and synthetic resources by building scripts/build_demo_counts.py independently. Changing a source, rule or included year after approval requires a new version, updated notes and an explicitly reviewed new pin.
