# Provo school coverage review, November 7, 2026

We compared the three UDOT public layers with the Provo City School District roster, the Utah State Board of Education open-campus directory, the Provo municipal polygon, and published school sources. A Provo postal address, a school name, and a route line crossing the city can each identify different sets of records. The release covers approved plans for schools in Provo and reviewed local supplements.

| Finding | Release decision |
| --- | --- |
| The district lists 13 neighborhood elementary schools and two middle schools. All 15 have school points in the district extract. | Preserve the original district layers. Compare each school's line and point counts separately; a school point alone does not establish route coverage. |
| Treeside Charter School (UDOT ID 773) | Seven UDOT route lines and the school point describe the **planned campus at 235 E 1600 S**. The school currently operates at **34 E 1700 S**. Use these lines for planning only. No walking or biking connection to the operating school entrance has been verified. The fourteen UDOT plan features remain under review. |
| Freedom Preparatory Academy and Walden School of Liberal Arts are open Provo charters in the state directory. Targeted statewide provider-name and geographic searches and school-source searches yielded no verified travel-plan geometry for their Provo campuses. | Keep both as open leads. Freedom's bus stops and the city trail near Walden serve different purposes. Request a dated school-issued route map if one becomes available. |
| Two Cherry Hill (Alpine) lines and one South Hills Middle School (Jordan) line intersect the municipal polygon. Neither school point lies in Provo. Some unrelated Franklin (Salt Lake) and Dixie Middle (Washington) records also carry Provo city labels. | Preserve these as boundary or provider attribution leads. Exclude from the Provo-school release until a source establishes that they serve a Provo school. |
| Wasatch Elementary published a separate map for its campus move. Provo Peaks publishes school travel material. Westridge council notes describe changes and recommendations. | Preserve the school materials as leads for segment-level comparison at already represented schools. Source identity, date, and reuse terms are needed before digitizing differences. |
| Slate Mountain operates youth-in-care education programs. The directory also lists private, online, early-learning, and secondary programs. | No verified additional school travel-route geometry found for those campuses in this audit. The result does not establish that these schools have no plans. |
