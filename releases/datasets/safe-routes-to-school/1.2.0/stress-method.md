# Bicycle traffic stress on Provo school plans

Lines describe modeled bicycling comfort along the published route. Points describe riding through a junction from a particular approach, or an identified plan crosswalk whose movement is unassessed. Estimated comfort applies to bicycling. School arrival traffic, walking conditions and current field conditions require separate evidence. Treeside’s routes serve its planned campus.

BikeWalk ran the PeopleForBikes Brokenspoke Analyzer 3.3.0 feature and stress stages on the November 7 Geofabrik Utah OpenStreetMap snapshot, using the November 8 Provo municipal boundary, a 2,680 m analysis buffer and a 25 mph residential model default. The results are BikeWalk estimates. The published Provo 26.04 city analysis was inspected as a comparison. Source hashes are in stress-inputs.json and sources.json. Utah Bikeways’ existing-network layer (last edited October 9) supplies a segment fallback.

Process route parts independently in UTM zone 12N. Evaluate intervals no longer than 5 m, comparing midpoint offset and local alignment to the source line. Eligible candidates lie within 12 m, project onto the interior, and align within approximately 30 degrees. Choose the lowest distance plus six times the alignment penalty; require a 3 m lead over the runner-up. Ambiguous BNA matches stay unassessed. A uniquely aligned Utah segment can cover a missing BNA match. Group consecutive equal matches by source and orientation. Boundaries are approximate to the sampling interval. Each original route part retains its length, including off-network and ambiguous pieces. Nearby parallel paths and small unmatched intervals can reduce coverage.

Score a through crossing only for consecutive BNA matches that share the same node, have at least three legs, pass within 12 m of that node and continue within approximately 30 degrees. The forward score belongs to the incoming way at its downstream endpoint; the reverse score belongs to the opposite incoming way. A one-way restriction suppresses the unavailable direction. A nearby UDOT marked crosswalk within 10 m of its school’s route is identified even when its movement is unknown. Associate its plan ID with a unique modeled crossing within 8 m, otherwise retain the plan point as unassessed. Plan geometry alone does not establish a crossing direction. Route endpoints, turns, ambiguous connections and intersections between separate route records can remain unassessed. A blank area on the map is not a complete inventory of crossings. Cross streets are names on other arms of the matched node.

Use lower for modeled 0–2 and higher for 3–4. Utah 0 is a valid facility score. Preserve the source’s raw category and directional BNA values. Display the higher available category when directions differ; show the missing direction in detail. Compass directions refer to the mapped movement and do not infer travel toward school. UDOT school membership and approval apply to the plan geometry, not the new estimates. Per-school length counts describe published route records and can overlap geographically. Counts of crossing records can include the same physical intersection on more than one plan route.
