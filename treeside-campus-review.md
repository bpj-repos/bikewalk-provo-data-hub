# Treeside campus and route review — November 7, 2026

The addresses resolve to three distinct uses. A [2019 transportation field study](https://www.westernite.org/datacollectionfund/2019/2019%20BYUite%20Western%20District%20Report%20FINAL%20060819.pdf) places the operating school at **1724 S State Street**, with primary access from State Street. [Treeside's current contact page](https://treesidecharterschool.org/contact-us) and the [2026–27 state school profile](https://datagateway.schools.utah.gov/Schools/7L110) give **34 E 1700 S**. The school lists Suite 200; the [state charter directory](https://ucap.schools.utah.gov/ContactDirectory/Index) lists Suite 132.

The [August 11 finance authority materials, PDF page 13](https://ucsfa.utah.gov/wp-content/uploads/08-11-2026-UCSFA-Meeting-Materials.pdf#page=13) specify **235 E 1600 South** for the proposed new campus. The [public hearing notice](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1101483.html) describes the acquisition at or near **255 E 1600 S**. UDOT's Treeside school record (ID 773, school object ID 491) gives **235 East 1600 South**, with plan approval dated October 5, 2026. This directly matches the financing memo. Treat the intended destination as the planned 1600 South site on that evidence; the public layers provide neither a verified pedestrian door nor an opening date. Do not label the seven lines as routes to the former State Street campus.
