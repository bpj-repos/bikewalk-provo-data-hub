## Baseline and contract

Count distinct state crash IDs. Use person types 4 (bicycle), 3 (pedestrian), 5 (other conveyance); only if none are present, use cyclist/pedestrian involvement flags. One crash counts once in total and in each applicable mode. Unmatched police and UDOT-only observations remain useful in the private collection but do not enlarge the public denominator. These are reported crashes, not people injured or a travel-risk rate.

## Implementation sequence and exit checks

1. **Inventory and freeze the handoff.** Pin the canonical producer commit, dependency versions, source registry, matching policy, normalization/derivation catalogs, schema, approved links and tests. Trace every module used by `danger_zones.update_incidents` (including `tool_hash` inputs), the aggregate exporter and each reader of the old private pointer. Inventory managed releases, predecessor chain, cumulative input state, original UDOT snapshots, portal/police deliveries, municipal street-network inputs and absolute paths needed to replay them. Record steward, access, checksums and authorized backup/restore location per input. Keep raw deliveries out of Git, deployment directories and public CI artifacts. **Exit:** a second operator restores and verifies the pinned source and both bundles in an isolated directory; existing production pointers are untouched. Do not assume counts/Safe Routes backup permissions extend to crash records.

2. **Transfer the existing updater without changing policy.** Move the relevant Python package modules, catalogs, tests and pinned dependencies into the hub as a private, separately installed tool; preserve its supported commands and explicit data roots. Do not run ingestion as part of a site build. Retain full UDOT layer checks for backfills; city portal workbook overlays on receipt; county export as reference; police workbook intake and local geocode uncertainty. Track last check, delivery dates, coverage and adoption independently. Partial deliveries and missing rows do not delete history. Preserve stable incident groups, absorbed-ID history, raw observations, reviewed links, source disagreements and enrichment. UDOT/portal joins require existing ID/time/location corroboration. Only unique, uncontested exact date/time/address and compatible-mode police matches can auto-link under the existing manual-update authorization; weak matches remain separate and contradictions affecting adopted identities block acceptance. **Exit:** shadow-run old and new producers on copies. Compare observations, groups, links, conflicts, enrichment and all annual rows; explain changes solely from tool identity/paths/timestamps. Test repeated receipt, historical correction, ambiguous match, changed policy, corrupted or incomplete input, concurrent update, interruption and rollback. Restore from a fresh path without editing immutable historical manifests. Do not leave two active writers.

3. **Build and review the first public candidate, offline.** Use the existing crash selection function and Projects’ annual-only allowlist; decouple it from the counter Parquet files required by the current combined research CLI. Pin a specific private manifest and verify its collection hash before reading. Export one complete CSV (nine rows at baseline), a typed dictionary, method/coverage/attribution notes and the hub’s checksummed manifest. Keep the chart JSON as a local projection unless there is a second use for publishing it. Only year, total, bicycle, pedestrian, other conveyance and full-calendar-year status belong in the table. No coordinates, IDs, narratives, case data, private receipts or enrichment. Validate uniqueness by year, overlap invariants, CSV rows and exact coverage against the baseline. Include a private before/after table by year and mode for future candidates, with changed source, rule or coverage identified. Record approval for the exact bytes and public reuse terms appropriate to this aggregate; prior approval covers annual output, not wider bulk detail or an inherited CC0 grant. **Exit:** independent offline rebuild yields identical public bytes, the 2025 example reproduces, and no counter data or private bundle reaches staging.

4. **Adopt and cut over.** Publish an immutable hub 1.0.0 via the existing static build/catalog; verify hashes, old dataset pins and anonymous downloads. Extend Projects’ existing hub status/stage/apply workflow; preserve the report URL and existing download URLs, four series, partial-year treatment and rollback. Verify all nine rows, chart, table and old links in staging. Keep Streets Watch’s crash-detail import, concern membership and rollback separate: it does not require annual aggregate adoption. Smoke-check any private pointer readers (including crash-details export, fatalities and incident-map workflows) against their contracts or keep their last verified pins. Retire only the duplicated producer after callers, maintainer access and rollback are verified. **Exit:** one hub writer, a restorable prior release, and Projects can adopt or roll back on its own.

## Recurring update and release decisions

Run on source receipt or explicit maintainer request initially. Receive and hash inputs → stage against a known predecessor → reconcile and review changes → accept the private collection → generate the annual candidate → release owner approves its exact pin → publish → Projects independently adopts. A private update that leaves the public rows, coverage and documented method unchanged needs no public revision. A failed update leaves the previous private pointer and public catalog usable. Test one subsequent private update and one public restore before retiring the previous production path. Use an existing authorized private backup and the existing static host; any new remote storage for restricted records needs a separate decision.

Recommended defaults: patch for corrected values under existing definitions; minor for a newly covered year; major for incompatible population, mode or completeness semantics. Do not mix a geography or source-population expansion into 1.0.0. The source steward confirms coverage and intake; the data maintainer runs reconciliation; the release owner approves public terms, pin and any change to the year-completeness rule; the Projects maintainer adopts. Record who may restore restricted inputs and who closes a partial year. Defer additional severity, person-level, monthly and geographic publications until there is an explicit use and disclosure review.

## Recovery milestone and second annual series

The [isolated recovery drill](crash-recovery.md) records the verified pins, dependency and caller inventory, and the reproducible writer and rollback drill.

The second independently versioned annual dataset is **Provo motor vehicle crashes with no nonmotorist participants**. The owner selected all motor vehicle types, including trucks and motorcycles. A qualifying crash may involve one vehicle or several. Both series should use the same versioned city portal deliveries and shared source parser. Use the complete city intake before the active transportation filter and deduplicate by state crash ID. Keep county exports in their reference role. On a corrected or overlapping city delivery, rebuild both candidates, compare each series by year, and let each release advance when its approved public output changes.

The retained city receipt beginning `3405efb3` contains 13,635 crash records. Its parsed person rows distinguish drivers, passengers, pedestrians, pedalcyclists and other pedestrians or conveyance users. There are 29 crash records without any linked person row. The membership rule needs affirmative evidence of motor-vehicle involvement and adequate participant coverage. Review person records alongside crash and rollup involvement flags, including conflicts and missing evidence. Track unresolved cases privately by year. Subtracting the active transportation total from all crashes would silently include unknown cases. Before publishing, verify how the portal records complete participant coverage. Fixtures should include truck-only and motorcycle-only crashes with all participants recorded as motorists, a truck crash involving a pedestrian, and a crash with insufficient participant evidence. The first two count, the pedestrian case is excluded, and the last remains unresolved. Review aggregate reuse terms and pin its release independently.
